Privacy Policy for CB Tuner
Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the CB Tuner application (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by cbsoftware00 as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use
   The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal identifiable information or user analytics.

Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO):
To function as an instrument tuner, the Application requires access to your device's microphone.

Purpose: The microphone is strictly used to listen to the real-time audio environment to detect the pitch of your instrument.

Local Processing: All audio processing and pitch detection occurs locally on your device in real-time.

No Recording or Transmission: The Application does not record, save, or transmit any audio data to remote servers, third parties, or cloud storage. Once the pitch is calculated, the raw audio data is instantly discarded from memory.

File Access (OpenDocument):
The Application requests access to your local file system solely when you choose to load custom microtonal tuning files (such as .scl files). The Application only reads the specific file you select and does not scan, collect, or transmit any other files on your device.

2. Third-Party Services
   The Application does not use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. There are no advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, or remote crash reporting tools embedded within the Application.

3. Data Retention
   Because the Application does not collect or transmit any personal data or audio recordings, no user data is retained by the developer. Custom settings and loaded tuning files are saved locally on your device using Android's standard SharedPreferences and remain strictly on your device.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
   We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
